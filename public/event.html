About the session

Bring your hardest role. We'll source it live.

This is a live sourcing session, not a product walkthrough. Attendees submit an open role when they register — on the call, we pick a few of those roles and build the actual search in real time: criteria, search, results, and the adjustments when the first pass doesn't land.
