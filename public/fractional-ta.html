The real problem

Most hiring teams don't have a sourcing problem. They have a system problem.

When the setup is wrong, adding recruiters, agencies or more candidates usually adds activity — not outcomes.

Signal 01

Critical roles stay open

Searches run for weeks, but the shortlist never becomes a confident hiring decision.

Signal 02

Hiring managers keep changing the brief

The role is live before the business has aligned on skills, trade-offs, level or evaluation criteria.

Signal 03

Good candidates disappear in the funnel

Slow feedback, unclear panels and inconsistent interviews create avoidable drop-off.

Signal 04

Offers are not converting

Compensation gets blamed while role narrative, timing, candidate motivation and closing discipline go unexamined.

Signal 05

Growth arrived before TA leadership

Founders, HR or recruiters are carrying hiring without a senior operator owning the system end to end.

Signal 06

Everyone is busy. Nobody owns the bottleneck.

Recruiting activity is visible; accountability for conversion, quality and speed is not.

Fit matters

This is not recruitment outsourcing.

Strong fit

  • High-growth technology company with important roles that need movement now.
  • Funding, expansion, new geography or a hiring surge has increased recruiting complexity.
  • Senior or specialist roles are repeatedly stalling despite recruiter effort.
  • The company needs senior TA judgment but is not ready for a permanent Head of TA.
  • Founders, HR and hiring leaders are willing to change broken hiring decisions — not just demand more CVs.

Not a fit

  • You only want an agency to forward resumes against a fixed JD.
  • There is no actual hiring priority, urgency or executive sponsorship.
  • The interview process cannot be changed regardless of evidence.
  • You want guaranteed hires or guaranteed joining dates.
  • The only buying criterion is the lowest possible recruiting fee.

FAQ

Before we talk.

Are you an external recruitment agency?

No. This offer is senior TA leadership and operating support. The work can include hands-on search execution, but the primary job is to diagnose and improve the hiring system around your business priorities.

Do you replace our internal recruiters?

Usually, no. The better use of a Fractional Head of TA is to make internal recruiters, hiring managers and leadership operate as one accountable hiring system. The exact scope depends on where the constraint sits.

Can you support technical and senior hiring?

That is where the model is strongest. My background includes software engineering, cloud, networking, security and multi-country technology hiring, including senior and specialist searches.

Do you guarantee hires?

No. Serious hiring work cannot ethically guarantee an individual's decision or joining. I can commit to a rigorous operating process, transparent funnel data, sharper decisions and measurable execution against agreed priorities.

What happens after 30 days?

You should have a clearer hiring system, working cadence, operating assets and a 30/60/90-day roadmap. We extend only when there is a defined business need and both sides believe continued fractional support creates value.

Can you work with companies outside India?

Yes. The operating experience is multi-country and the Fractional TA opportunity set is global. Scope, time-zone overlap and commercial terms are agreed before the engagement begins.

Where is your hiring actually breaking?

If the answer is unclear, that is exactly where the diagnostic starts. Bring the priority roles, the funnel and the current blockers. We will identify whether there is a real Fractional TA problem worth solving.
