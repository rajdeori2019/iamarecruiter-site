When this model makes sense

Your recruiters may not be the problem.

If the business has activity but not enough movement, first identify the constraint. More sourcing against the same broken setup usually creates more noise.

01

Critical roles keep ageing

Weeks pass, interviews happen, but the role never reaches a confident decision.

02

The brief keeps moving

Level, must-haves, compensation or location shift after the search has already started.

03

The funnel leaks

Good candidates disappear between screening, panels, feedback, offer and joining.

04

No senior owner owns the system

Founders, HR, recruiters and managers are all involved, but nobody owns the bottleneck end to end.

Qualification

This is not recruitment outsourcing.

Strong fit

  • You have important technology roles that need movement now.
  • Funding, expansion, a new geography or hiring surge has increased complexity.
  • Senior or specialist searches repeatedly stall despite recruiter activity.
  • You need senior TA judgment before you need a permanent Head of TA.
  • Leadership is willing to change a broken process when evidence shows the problem.

Not a fit

  • You only want an agency to forward CVs against a fixed JD.
  • There is no real hiring priority or executive sponsorship.
  • The interview process cannot change regardless of evidence.
  • You require guaranteed hires or guaranteed joining dates.
  • The only decision criterion is the lowest recruiting fee.

FAQ

Before we talk.

Are you an external recruitment agency?

No. This offer is senior TA leadership and operating support. It can include hands-on search execution, but the primary job is to diagnose and improve the hiring system around business priorities.

Do you replace our internal recruiters?

Usually, no. The model is designed to improve how recruiters, hiring managers and leadership operate together. Scope changes only when the actual constraint requires additional execution capacity.

Do you guarantee hires?

No. Candidate decisions and joining cannot be ethically guaranteed. The commitment is to a rigorous operating process, transparent funnel diagnosis and agreed execution against priority hiring problems.

Can you work with companies outside India?

Yes. The operating background is APAC and multi-country technology hiring. Time-zone overlap, scope and commercials are agreed before work begins.
