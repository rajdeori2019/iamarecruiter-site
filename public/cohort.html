The gap

AI adoption is not the same as strategic recruiting.

Writing faster emails, JDs and Boolean strings may improve execution speed. It does not automatically improve judgment, market diagnosis or stakeholder advisory.

Gap 01

You execute requirements you should be diagnosing

The role goes live before talent availability, compensation, location, skills and trade-offs are challenged.

Gap 02

You bring candidates, not a recommendation

Hiring managers receive profiles and updates, but not enough evidence to make better hiring decisions.

Gap 03

AI output looks useful — until it is wrong

Without verification, privacy, bias and hallucination controls, faster output can create faster mistakes.

Gap 04

Your profile shows activity, not strategic proof

Requisitions filled do not show whether you can diagnose a market, design an assessment or advise leadership.

The horizontal layer

AI governance is not an optional module.

Every pillar is taught with verification, privacy, bias and hallucination controls because strategic recruiters cannot advise a business using evidence they have not checked.

Verification

Know what requires source validation before it becomes a recommendation.

Hallucination control

Separate AI-generated hypotheses from facts you can defend with evidence.

Bias

Use AI without quietly hard-coding unfair or irrelevant screening criteria into hiring decisions.

Privacy

Understand what candidate, employee and company data should not be exposed casually to AI tools.

FAQ

Before you apply.

Is this a beginner recruitment course?

No. It is designed for working recruiters who already understand recruitment fundamentals and want to move toward more analytical, consultative and business-facing work.

Is this mainly a ChatGPT or prompt-engineering course?

No. AI is used as an accelerator across talent intelligence, diagnosis, sourcing, assessment and analytics. The core skill being developed is recruiter judgment.

What will I have at the end?

You should have six applied recruiting artifacts plus a capstone proof-of-work exercise. The value is in the work you can explain and use, not simply attendance.

Will this help me get a job or promotion?

The program is designed to improve strategic recruiting capability, but it does not guarantee a job, interview, promotion, salary increase or employer decision.

Why is the cohort capped at 10?

The format includes applied work, review and role-play. A small cohort keeps participation and feedback practical instead of turning the program into a webinar.

What exactly is the refund policy?

You can request a 100% refund within 48 hours after Session 1 ends. Session 1 is scheduled for 19 September 2026, 10:30 AM–12:30 PM IST, so the Batch 01 refund deadline is 12:30 PM IST on 21 September 2026. Refund requests after that deadline are not eligible.

Do I need paid AI tools?

No specific paid-tool requirement is being advertised. Final tool guidance will be shared before the cohort; unnecessary subscriptions will not be forced where the learning objective can be achieved with available options.

Will I receive a certificate?

The cohort is currently sold on capability and proof of work, not on a certificate promise. Any certificate policy will be stated in the final onboarding terms before payment.

What if I miss a live session?

This is an implementation-led cohort, so live participation is strongly preferred. Recording, make-up or catch-up arrangements will follow the final onboarding terms.
