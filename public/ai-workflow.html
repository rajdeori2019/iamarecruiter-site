STRONG RECRUITING STARTS WITH A BETTER QUESTION.
Before sourcing

Do not only ask, “Where can I find candidates?”

Also ask: “Is the market telling us this requirement is realistic?”

The goal is not to become a market researcher. The goal is to make a better-informed recruiting recommendation before execution begins.

Why not just ask ChatGPT?

AI can generate an answer. That does not automatically make it reliable market intelligence.

A confident-looking answer still needs source quality, recency, role context, evidence checks and recruiter judgment.

AI says a talent pool is large.

The statement may sound convincing.

You verify what was actually searched.

Define the role, location, source and observable evidence.

AI gives a salary number.

The number may be an unsupported estimate.

You trace the compensation evidence.

Keep sources, assumptions and uncertainty visible.

AI recommends a hiring approach.

A recommendation can be generated without sufficient context.

You own the recruiter recommendation.

Use verified evidence and state what remains unknown.

FAQ

Before you start

Is this an eBook?

No. The guide is supporting material. The main experience is the guided Talent Market Snapshot Challenge and reusable toolkit.

Do I need ChatGPT Plus?

No specific paid AI plan is required for the concept itself. Use an AI tool available to you, subject to your employer’s policies and privacy requirements.

Can I use a real hiring requirement?

Yes. That is the purpose of the second part. Use only information you are authorized to work with, and avoid uploading confidential or personal data into public AI tools without appropriate permission.

Will you give me a completed market report for my role?

No. The challenge teaches you how to research, verify and structure the evidence. It does not promise a pre-built market report for every role.

Is the market data guaranteed to be accurate?

No. Market information changes, sources vary in quality, and AI can produce unsupported claims. Verification and clear limitations are part of the workflow.

How is this different from free prompts?

Prompts are only one input. The challenge focuses on the complete process: research → evidence → verification → feasibility → recruiter recommendation.

Is this the full Recruiter Cohort?

No. This is a small entry experience around AI-Powered Talent Intelligence. The cohort goes deeper into research, verification, stakeholder advisory and complete Talent Market Intelligence Reports.

What happens after I finish?

You can keep using the Talent Market Snapshot structure on future hiring requirements. The broader Strategic Recruiter Diagnostic and cohort remain separate next steps.
