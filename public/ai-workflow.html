The problem

Using an LLM is easy. Using it responsibly in a hiring decision is harder.

The market research behind this beta shows broad AI adoption in recruiting, while screening confidence, verification, bias, governance and quality-of-hire capability remain meaningful gaps. The challenge is built around that gap — not around access to ChatGPT itself.

Gap 01

The challenge method

“Show the evidence, uncertainty and what I must verify.”

Criterion → Evidence present / partial / missing / ambiguous → Verification question → Recruiter judgment
  • Criteria are clarified before evaluation.
  • Every conclusion points back to evidence.
  • Uncertainty stays visible.
  • The final hiring recommendation remains human-owned.
How it works

One realistic hiring case. Six practical decisions.

The founding beta uses a synthetic hiring case so you can practice the workflow without sharing confidential candidate or company data.

01 · Clarify

Interrogate the requirement

Use AI to separate explicit requirements, preferences, vague language and questions that still need hiring-manager clarification.

→ Lock criteria before screening
02 · Calibrate

Define what actually counts

Translate the agreed requirement into must-haves, preferred areas, transferable skills and business outcomes.

→ Stop treating every keyword equally
03 · Evaluate

Extract candidate evidence

Ask AI to classify each criterion as evidence present, partial/transferable, missing or ambiguous — with the source behind each conclusion.

→ Evidence before impression
04 · Verify

Expose uncertainty

Generate the questions needed to test what the CV does not prove instead of letting AI silently fill the gaps.

→ Hypothesis is not fact
05 · Compare

Use the same criteria for everyone

Build a side-by-side evidence matrix without arbitrary total match percentages or prestige-based shortcuts.

→ Consistent comparison
06 · Advise

Prepare an HM recommendation

Turn candidate evidence into a concise recommendation that separates facts, recruiter judgment, unresolved risk and the next action.

→ Profiles become reasoning
The operating method
CriteriaEvidenceUncertaintyVerificationHuman judgmentRecommendation
AI generates hypotheses and structures information.

It can help extract, organize and compare what is present in the inputs.

Evidence determines what can be defended.

If the input does not establish something, the workflow keeps it as missing or ambiguous instead of converting it into a fact.

The recruiter owns the decision.

The challenge does not teach autonomous hiring decisions or promise that an AI score predicts candidate performance.

The recommendation is business-facing.

The final step is not “here are three CVs.” It is a structured explanation of what the evidence supports and what still needs validation.

FAQ

Before you buy.

Why would I pay when I already have ChatGPT or Gemini?

You are not buying access to an LLM. The challenge gives you a predefined recruiter workflow, evidence categories, verification logic, comparison format and HM recommendation structure so you do not have to invent the method every time.

Is this an AI CV-screening tool?

No. It is a guided learning and workflow product. It does not autonomously rank applicants, make hiring decisions or claim that an AI-generated score predicts job performance.

Do I need to upload real candidate CVs?

No. The founding beta uses a synthetic case specifically so you can practice without sharing confidential candidate data. If you later reuse the method at work, follow your employer’s privacy, security and AI-use policies.

Which AI tool do I need?

No particular paid AI tool is required for the founding beta. The workflow is designed around general LLM capability rather than one vendor-specific feature set.

Does this guarantee better hires or faster hiring?

No. The challenge is designed to teach a more structured way to use AI-supported evidence and verification. It does not guarantee hiring outcomes, recruiter performance, promotions, salary increases or employment results.

Why is payment handled on WhatsApp?

This is a founding V0 validation batch. We are intentionally keeping checkout simple while validating whether working recruiters pay for and use the workflow. The ₹99 payment path and access instructions are shared directly after you message us.

What happens after the challenge?

You can take the Strategic Recruiter Readiness Diagnostic. If the result shows meaningful gaps and you want deeper applied development, you can then explore the AI-Enabled Strategic Talent Advisor cohort. There is no obligation to join the cohort.
