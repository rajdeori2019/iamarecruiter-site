Responsible AI

AI supports the research. It does not turn guesses into market facts.

The workflow is designed to keep the recruiter responsible for verification and interpretation.

  • Keep source links for factual market claims.
  • Label estimates and uncertainty clearly.
  • Do not invent talent-pool counts or compensation numbers.
  • Do not treat one source as the entire market.
  • Do not upload confidential or personal data without appropriate permission.
  • Use AI to structure and analyze evidence — not to manufacture it.

FAQ

Before you start

Is this an AI prompt pack?

No. AI prompts may be used inside the guided workflow, but the product is structured around research questions, evidence, verification and a usable Talent Market Snapshot.

Do I need a live hiring role?

You can learn from the guided case without one. The second part is more practical if you have a current hiring requirement you can safely use.

Is this the full cohort Module 1?

No. It is an entry experience related to Module 1. The cohort goes deeper and the Module 1 deliverable is a full Talent Market Intelligence Report.

Does the ₹99 challenge guarantee better hiring outcomes?

No. It is a learning and workflow product. It does not guarantee hires, promotions, job outcomes or business results.

Can I use confidential company or candidate data?

Only if you have appropriate permission and your employer's policies allow it. Otherwise use anonymized or synthetic information.
