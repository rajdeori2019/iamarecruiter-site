Module 2 · Target Company Mapping

Map the right talent pools

Build a defensible source-company map. AI suggestions start as research candidates. A company becomes verified only when you record evidence supporting why it belongs in the search.

1. Generate research candidates

Use the locked requirement and recruiter-verified search lanes. If your AI tool has web access, it must use reliable current sources and cite them. Without evidence, companies remain research candidates.

Hard distinction: Potential source company ≠ verified source company.

2. Recruiter verification

For each retained company, connect it to the requirement and record the evidence. Remove companies you cannot justify.
