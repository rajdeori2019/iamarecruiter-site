The real problem

Strong experience can still sound too junior, too vague or too generic in an interview.

The issue is often not a lack of experience. It is whether the interviewer can clearly see your individual ownership, judgment, business impact and level.

Signal 01

You answer at task level

You explain what the team did, but not the decisions you personally owned or the trade-offs you drove.

Signal 02

Your strongest evidence is buried

The answer is long, but the interviewer has to work too hard to find the decision, scale, impact or learning.

Signal 03

Your stories are not calibrated to the target level

A strong Manager story may still be weak evidence for a Director or Principal-level role.

Signal 04

You keep preparing more questions instead of diagnosing the gap

More interview content does not solve a positioning, evidence or communication problem.

7-part diagnostic

Before coaching the answer, diagnose what is actually breaking.

The diagnostic separates symptoms from the underlying interview-conversion constraint.

01 · Funnel

Where does the process break?

Interview stage, repeated rejection pattern and whether the problem is actually conversion.

02 · Role

What is the role really evaluating?

Target responsibilities, business context, likely competencies and role-specific expectations.

03 · Level

What does the seniority require?

Scope, judgment, influence, decision-making and impact expected at the target level.

04 · Evidence

What proof are you using?

Whether your stories contain specific ownership, scale, decisions and measurable or observable outcomes.

05 · Leadership

How do you show leadership?

Stakeholder influence, conflict, trade-offs, team leadership, ambiguity and accountability.

06 · Communication

Can the interviewer follow the answer?

Structure, concision, relevance, clarity and how quickly the answer reaches the decision and impact.

07 · Calibration

Does the evidence match the role?

Whether your strongest examples actually signal the level and competencies you are targeting.

Output

A diagnosis — not vague feedback

Primary constraint, secondary constraint, evidence, what is not the primary problem, fit, intervention and next action.

The method
