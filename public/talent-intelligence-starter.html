AI + verification

Why not just ask ChatGPT for the market?

AI can help structure analysis. It does not automatically turn a confident answer into verified talent-market evidence.

AI can help you

  • organise a hiring requirement;
  • generate clarification questions;
  • structure search lanes;
  • compare evidence you provide;
  • draft a recruiter recommendation.

You still need to verify

  • candidate or search-result counts;
  • compensation observations;
  • company facts and relevance;
  • talent availability conclusions;
  • the final recruiter recommendation.
Evidence rule: separate Fact / Inference / Assumption / Unknown. If evidence is missing, keep it as “Unknown — requires verification.”

What exactly do I receive?

The Starter Pack is structured around four core assets: the Stop Sourcing Blind Playbook, Talent Intelligence Workbook, Recruiter AI Prompt Sheet and Worked Example, plus the Talent Market Snapshot application tool.

Is this just an eBook?

No. The playbook teaches the method, but the package also includes the workbook, reusable AI prompts, worked example and application tool.

Do I need ChatGPT Plus?

No specific paid AI subscription is required to read or use the pack. If you choose to use the AI prompts, availability and usage limits depend on the AI tool and plan you use.

Can I use a real hiring requirement?

Yes, if you are authorised to work with it. Do not enter confidential company information or candidate personal data into an AI tool unless your organisation permits it.

Will the pack give me verified live market data automatically?

No. Live market evidence needs to come from real sourcing, compensation or company sources available to you. The pack helps you structure, record, question and interpret that evidence without treating AI-generated numbers as market truth.

Is this the full Recruiter Cohort?

No. This is a focused Talent Intelligence Starter Pack for one workflow. It is not the full cohort program.

Does this guarantee a hire, faster time-to-fill or accurate salary data?

No. The pack provides a structured recruiting workflow and verification guardrails. It does not guarantee candidate availability, hiring outcomes, time-to-fill or perfect market data.
