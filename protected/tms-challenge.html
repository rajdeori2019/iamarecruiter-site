BUILD YOUR TALENT MARKET SNAPSHOT.

Bring one live role. Work through the market before you start sourcing. Keep evidence separate from assumptions and finish with a recruiter recommendation.

1. Hiring Requirement

Write the role, location, experience range, must-have skills, seniority and domain. Split materially different profiles into separate lanes.

Output: one clear search lane with must-haves, nice-to-haves and explicit unknowns.

2. Talent Supply

Estimate the searchable pool using evidence you can verify. Record the filters and source used. Do not present an AI-generated count as a fact unless you can reproduce it.

Decision question: Is the pool broad enough for volume sourcing, or narrow enough to require direct mapping?

3. Target Companies

Map direct-fit companies first, then adjacent pools. Explain why each pool is relevant to the role rather than dumping company names.

4. Location View

Compare the requested location with realistic adjacent talent hubs. Separate candidate availability from relocation assumptions.

5. Compensation Evidence

Use the strongest available evidence first: employer-published ranges, candidate-reported CTC data, then credible salary platforms. Label every source and date.

6. Hiring Feasibility

Choose one label: Realistic · Difficult · Narrow · Misaligned · Still unclear. State why.

7. Recruiter Recommendation

Turn the market evidence into an action: geography, sourcing approach, target companies, compensation calibration, hiring-manager decisions and what must be validated next.

8. Evidence & Unknowns

List what is verified, what is directional and what remains unknown. The snapshot should help a hiring manager make a decision, not create false precision.
