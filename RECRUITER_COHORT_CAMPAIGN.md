# Recruiter Cohort Campaign — Locked Core Message

## Status
Locked on 9 Sep 2026 for the I AM A RECRUITER Cohort launch campaign.

## Core Campaign Problem
Recruiters are using AI, but the stronger question is whether AI is actually making them more strategic recruiters.

Do not position the market problem as "recruiters do not know ChatGPT" or "recruiters do not know AI." That is too basic and weak for this offer.

The campaign should focus on the gap between using AI for tasks and using AI to build strategic recruiting capability.

## Locked Hook
**Recruiters are using AI. But is AI actually making you a more strategic recruiter?**

## Locked Campaign Message
You may already use ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or other AI tools for sourcing, JDs, outreach and candidate summaries.

But where are recruiters still struggling?

- Talent intelligence?
- Hiring diagnosis?
- Stakeholder advisory?
- Sourcing strategy?
- Recruiting analytics?
- Skills-based assessment?
- Responsible AI?

Before we finalize the **I AM A RECRUITER Cohort launching 3 October 2026**, we want recruiters and TA professionals to tell us what they genuinely want to learn — and what they would realistically invest in it.

## Survey Bridge
The survey should not tell respondents what their problem is. The campaign can raise the hypothesis, but the survey should let the market reveal the actual need.

Preferred framing:

**Recruiters are using AI — but where is it still not helping enough?**

Then let the survey capture:
- what they want to upskill in over the next 5–6 months
- where they currently feel stuck
- which AI tools they already use
- what they still struggle with despite AI
- preferred learning format
- realistic investment range
- what would make a program worth paying for

## Pricing Question — Locked Wording
**If a program genuinely solved the problems you selected and included practical live guidance, what would feel like a realistic investment?**

## Campaign Objective
Use the campaign for three outcomes at the same time:
1. Market research
2. Lead qualification
3. Cohort demand and pricing validation

## Branding Rules
All campaign creatives should use only the **I AM A RECRUITER** brand system:
- I AM A RECRUITER logo
- approved digital model assets
- visual language aligned with www.iamarecruiter.in
- yellow / black / white brand direction
- no VantaHire or AI Revo Labs branding in this campaign

## Channel Execution Rule
Create channel-specific creative and copy one by one rather than posting one identical asset everywhere.

Planned channels:
- LinkedIn
- WhatsApp groups
- Email database
- Instagram
- Facebook
- WhatsApp Status
- YouTube

## Core Strategic Principle
**Using AI ≠ becoming a Strategic Recruiter.**

The campaign should move through:
**Problem → Curiosity / research tension → Survey → Market evidence → Cohort positioning**

Do not overclaim what recruiters need before enough real responses are collected.
